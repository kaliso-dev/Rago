# Logos

- `rago-mark.svg`: the R in `red` #FF3B30, the source of every other file. Default on any ground.
- `rago-mark-white.svg`: the R in `ink` #F5F5F6, only on a red ground or a photo.
- `rago-app-icon.svg`: red R on transparent, for the taskbar, the .exe and Windows icons.
- `rago-app-tile.svg`: red R on a dark rounded tile (56px corners on 256), where an opaque square is required (avatars, apple-touch icon).
- `rago-favicon.svg`: red R nearly full frame, for sizes under 32px.
- `rago-wordmark-white.svg` (ink #F5F5F6 letters, red R) and `rago-wordmark-ink.svg` (#0A0A0B letters, red R): the earlier "Rago Optimizer" wordmark set in Geist. Site and installer only, not in the app, where the lockup is the R followed by "AGO" in Barlow Condensed.

## Printed app icon (22/11)

- `rago-app-printed-dark.svg`: the app icon, chosen on 22/11. The R in `paper` with the `red` layer 9.5 right and down, rough edges, on a dark tile with halftone dots in the top right. The tile keeps it visible on a light Windows taskbar.
- `rago-app-printed-red.svg`: the louder alternative, `paper` R with a black layer on a `red` tile. Not in use; kept in case the installer or a store page needs it.
- `rago-app-printed-16.svg`: the 16px build. No texture under 64px, no red layer at 16px, the R takes 95% of the tile so it stays readable.
- The flat `rago-app-icon.svg` and `rago-app-tile.svg` stay for places that need one flat colour.
