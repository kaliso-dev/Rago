in-game barOver your game: FPS, ping, CPU, GPU, RAM and live tweaks. The game needs to run borderless or windowed.

stay by the clock when closedClosing the window keeps Rago running, with its icon by the clock. Quit from the icon to close it.
start with windowsOn by default: Rago starts hidden by the clock when you sign in to Windows, so the in-game bar is ready.

while you play

Rago does this when a game starts and undoes it when it ends, as long as it's open or by the clock.

high-performance graphics card for gamesYour PC has a single graphics card: nothing to do here.
high performance power mode while you playFrom Balanced or Power saver, plugged in only. Your mode comes back when the game ends, unless you picked another one meanwhile.
