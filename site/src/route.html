The real problem

One lost packet stops everything behind it.

Minecraft Java plays every packet in order. If packet 4 goes missing, packets 5, 6 and 7 still arrive, but your game can't use them until 4 is sent again. So it sits still, then catches up all at once.

A faster internet plan doesn't change that. The wait comes from the loss, not from your speed. To stop the freeze, the missing packet has to be there before your game needs it.

  • A single loss is usually sent again quickly, and you barely see it.
  • A burst of losses, like a Wi-Fi hiccup, stalls the game much longer.
  • That's what Rago Route goes after: the losses, before they reach your game.

01Dual path

Two roads. The first copy wins.

ProblemOne lost or late packet on your road and your game waits for it.
FixEvery packet leaves on two roads at once: straight to Rago's exit in Paris, and through Rago's relay in Frankfurt. The first copy in is used, the other is dropped. One road can stumble without your game waiting for it.

02Backup packets

A lost packet, rebuilt on arrival.

New
ProblemWhen a packet is lost, asking for it again costs a full round trip, and your game waits all that time.
FixFor every small group of packets, up to 4, Rago Route adds one backup packet made from all of them. If one of them goes missing, it's rebuilt on the spot from the others and the backup. No asking, no waiting.
Lab test: 20 MB sent through 20% packet loss on each road, and 1,413 packets repaired on arrival, never sent again. Simulated network on our servers, no game, Nov 8, 2026.
03Quick resend

Still missing? Sent again, fast.

ProblemNormally a lost packet is sent again only once your connection notices it's gone, and a burst of losses can stall your game for a long time.
FixRago Route numbers every packet. As soon as a later one lands, the gap is spotted and the missing packet is sent again, about one round trip later, without waiting for a timeout.
04Session hold

Your Wi-Fi blinks. The exit holds on.

ProblemWhen the signal cuts out, your connection freezes, and when it comes back it can take seconds more to catch up.
FixRago's exit keeps your connection to the server open while your PC is cut off, for up to a minute. The moment your network is back, everything that's missing goes out at once. A long cut can still end your match: the server expects your game to answer now and then.
Lab test: an 8-second total cut, and traffic flowing again within 20 ms of the network coming back. No game, Nov 7, 2026.

What it won't do.

We'd rather tell you now than after you pay. Rago Route is made for connections that drop packets.

  • Shorten the distance. Hypixel's servers are in North America. From Europe, your ping stays about the same: 113 against 119 ms in our test from France.

Questions

Clear answers.

Will it lower my ping?

Usually not by much, and that isn't its job. Rago Route goes after the losses and freezes on the way to the server. Your average ping depends mostly on distance: Hypixel's servers are in North America, so from Europe it stays about the same. In our test from France, the median was 113 ms through Rago Route against 119 ms direct, over 79 pings taken at the same moment, with the prototype on a Mac.

When will I feel a difference?

When your connection drops packets: on Wi-Fi, at busy hours, or on a line that spikes. On a clean line there's little to fix, so you'll feel little. In our own test with simulated packet loss, the difference was hard to feel at 5% loss and obvious at 25%. Rago Optimizer's free network test shows your loss and spikes to Hypixel and Minemen Club.

Is it a VPN?

Not a full VPN. Only your Minecraft connection goes through it, and only when you join localhost: the rest of your PC's traffic doesn't change. But as with a VPN, Hypixel sees the address of Rago's server in Paris instead of yours.

Can Hypixel block my account if I use it?

Hypixel sees the address of Rago's server in Paris, which other Rago Route players share. We're testing it with several accounts before saying more here.

Is it fair play?

Rago Route doesn't touch Minecraft: no mod, nothing injected, no fake ping, no lag switch. It carries your game's own packets, encrypted, by more than one road, and delivers them in order. It doesn't change what you send, add anything to the game or touch other players, and it doesn't change how the server counts your hits. Your match stays encrypted by Minecraft, end to end with the server, so Rago Route can't read it or change it.

Which servers does it work with?

Hypixel, on Minecraft Java, from Windows 10 and 11. And Minemen Club (EU): join localhost:25566. Tell us on our Discord which server you want next.

Does it work outside Europe?

It's made for players in Europe and tested from France so far: your game goes through Paris. From North America, Hypixel is already close, so the detour wouldn't help.

How do I use it?

Start your trial and install Rago Optimizer. Open Network, then Rago Route, and turn it on. In Minecraft, add the server localhost for Hypixel and join it. The tab shows your roads and counters while you play.

What if Rago's server is down?

Then Rago Route can't connect, and a match going through it stops. Keep your usual Hypixel address in your server list: turn Rago Route off and join it as usual.

How do I add my phone?

Share your phone's connection with your PC: plug it in with a USB cable and turn on Personal Hotspot (iPhone) or USB tethering (Android), or pair it over Bluetooth. If your PC is wired to your router, it can also join the phone's hotspot over Wi-Fi. Rago Route finds the phone and adds it as a road. If it can't, the tab shows which step is missing, like a driver or a setting, and how to fix it. A cable that only charges can't work: it has no data wires.

How much mobile data does the phone use?

About 70 to 90 MB per hour of play, as an estimate. In our test on the Mac prototype, the phone received 11.2 MB in 10 minutes on Hypixel, before backup packets were added, and they add up to a quarter more. The app shows the data received, live.

What if my phone's signal is weak?

Then that road is slow. While your Wi-Fi works, it barely matters: the fastest copy wins. When your Wi-Fi cuts out completely, your game can only go as fast as your phone. In our Wi-Fi cut test on the prototype with a weak 4G signal, the game stayed connected, but ping rose above one second until the Wi-Fi came back.

What happens if my internet cuts out completely?

Rago's exit keeps your connection to the server open while your PC is cut off, for up to a minute, and sends what's missing the moment you're back. In our lab test, traffic flowed again within 20 ms of the network coming back after an 8-second total cut. A long cut can still end your match: the server expects your game to answer now and then.

What does Rago see?

Rago Route encrypts and signs every packet between your PC and Rago's exit. It doesn't read your match, which Minecraft encrypts end to end with the server. To let you in, Rago's servers check a short pass from your account that names your license and nothing else.
