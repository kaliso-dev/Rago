How it works

One lost packet freezes everything behind it.

Minecraft Java plays packets in order: when one goes missing, the ones behind it wait until it's sent again, and your game freezes, then jumps. A faster plan doesn't change that. Rago Route gets the missing packet there before your game needs it. The drawings below are illustrations; the line under each one is what we measured.

01Two roads

Every packet leaves on two roads at once: straight to Rago's exit in Paris, and through Rago's relay in Frankfurt. The first copy in is used, so one road can stumble without your game waiting.

2,246 packets saved by the second road in about two minutes, with 30% dropped on purpose on the first. Hypixel, prototype on a Mac, Nov 6, 2026.

02Backup packets

For every group of up to 4 packets, one backup packet made from all of them travels too. If one of them is lost, it's rebuilt on arrival from the others, with no round trip.

1,413 packets rebuilt on arrival, never sent again: 20 MB through 20% loss on each road. Lab, no game, Nov 8, 2026.

03Quick resend

Both copies lost? Every packet is numbered, so the gap is spotted as soon as a later one lands, and the missing packet is sent again in about one round trip, without waiting for a timeout.

Counted live in the app: every packet sent again shows in the Resends counter of the Rago Route tab.

04Session hold

Your Wi-Fi blinks: Rago's exit keeps your connection to the server open for up to a minute, then sends everything missing the moment you're back. A long cut can still end a match: the server expects your game to answer.

20 ms to flow again after an 8-second total cut of the network. Lab, no game, Nov 7, 2026.

05Phone backupBETA

Share your phone's connection over USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi: its mobile data becomes one more road, used at the same time as your home connection. When your Wi-Fi stalls, the phone's copy gets there first. Rago checks each step and tells you what's missing.

About 30 s of Wi-Fi switched off, and the game stayed connected over 4G, but unplayable on a weak signal. Hypixel, prototype on a Mac, Nov 7, 2026.

06Live counters

The Rago Route tab in the app shows each road live: its round trip, how often it arrived first, its losses and its data, plus every packet saved, rebuilt or sent again. On a clean line it says so too.

0 packets to save on our first evening on Windows: the direct road lost nothing, and the tab said so. Hypixel, Nov 7, 2026.

Questions

Clear answers.

Will it lower my ping?

Usually not by much, and that isn't its job. Rago Route goes after the losses and freezes on the way to the server. Your average ping depends mostly on distance: from France, the median to Hypixel was 113 ms through Rago Route and 119 ms direct, over 79 pings taken at the same moment.

When will I feel a difference?

When your connection drops packets: on Wi-Fi, at busy hours, or on a line that spikes. On a clean line there's little to fix. The free network test in Rago Optimizer shows your loss and spikes to Hypixel and Minemen Club.

Is it a VPN?

Not a full VPN. Only your Minecraft connection goes through it, and only when you join localhost: the rest of your PC's traffic doesn't change. But as with a VPN, Hypixel sees the address of Rago's server in Paris instead of yours.

Is it fair play?

Rago Route doesn't touch Minecraft: no mod, nothing injected, no fake ping, no lag switch. It carries your game's own packets, encrypted, by more than one road, and delivers them in order. Your match stays encrypted by Minecraft, end to end with the server, so Rago Route can't read it or change it.

Can Hypixel block my account if I use it?

Hypixel sees the address of Rago's server in Paris, which other Rago Route players share. We're testing it with several accounts before saying more here.

Choose Add Rago Route above, sign in and pay $3 a month or $17.99 a year on Stripe, with the card of your Premium. You can also add it from your account page, or from the Rago Route tab of the app. It's its own subscription: your Premium doesn't change.

Taken without it, Rago Route stops when the trial ends, and you can add it later from your account. Cancel before the end and you pay nothing.

What happens if I cancel Premium?

How much mobile data does the phone use?

About 70 to 90 MB per hour of play, as an estimate: in our test on the prototype, the phone received 11.2 MB in 10 minutes on Hypixel, and backup packets add up to a quarter more. The app shows the data received, live.

What if Rago's server is down?

Then Rago Route can't connect, and a match going through it stops. Keep your usual Hypixel address in your server list: turn Rago Route off and join it as usual.

No, payments aren't refunded: that's what the free trial is for. You can cancel anytime from your account and keep what you paid for until the end of its period. If you're charged twice or by mistake, contact us and we give back the extra payment.
