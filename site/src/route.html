How it works

Rago Optimizer tunes your PC. Rago Route takes the road.

Five fixes that start where your PC ends, built together into one engine.

01Dual path

Two roads. First one wins.

ProblemOne lost packet on your road and the game waits while it's sent again.
FixEvery packet leaves on two roads at once. The first copy to arrive is used, the other is dropped. One road can stumble and your game never knows.
Tested on Hypixel: with 30% of packets dropped on purpose on one road, the other road rescued 2,246 of them in about two minutes. The match went on.
02Instant repair

A lost packet is back in one round trip.

ProblemWhen Wi-Fi drops a burst of packets, everything behind the gap waits for them, sometimes for a long time.
FixRago Route numbers every packet. As soon as a later one lands, the gap is spotted and the missing packet is sent again, without waiting for a timeout.
Lab test: 20 MB sent through 20% packet loss on both roads arrived intact, byte for byte.
03Session hold

Your Wi-Fi drops. Your match holds.

ProblemWhen the signal cuts out, your connection freezes. When it comes back, it can take several more seconds to catch up.
FixRago's exit keeps your connection to the server open for up to a minute while your PC is silent. The moment your network is back, what's missing is sent straight away.
Lab test: an 8-second total cut, nothing lost, traffic flowing again within 20 ms of the network coming back.
04Phone backup

Your phone becomes your backup road.

Testing now
ProblemWi-Fi is the weakest link in most setups. A wall, a busy channel or a neighbor's router is enough for a spike.
FixPlug in your phone and Rago Route uses its mobile data as a second road, at the same time as your Wi-Fi. When the Wi-Fi stalls, the phone's copy gets there first. You see the mobile data it uses, live.
05Smart route

The best road, picked for you.

Coming with the app
ProblemPicking a proxy by hand means guessing, and the best road changes from one evening to the next.
FixRago measures the roads from your PC to your server, direct and through each Rago exit, and sends you down the fastest one. When your direct road is already the best, Rago keeps it.
Fair play

Faster delivery. The same game.

Rago Route only carries your connection. It never fakes your ping and never holds packets back for an edge. It can't read your match either: once you're logged in, Minecraft encrypts everything between your game and the server, and Rago Route passes it along untouched.

Questions

Straight answers.

Will it lower my ping?

Sometimes, but that's not the main goal. Rago Route removes spikes, packet loss and freezes. Your average ping only goes down when your usual route takes a detour. In our first tests from France, Rago Route was a few milliseconds faster than the direct route to Hypixel.

Is it a VPN?

No. Rago Route carries your Minecraft connection and nothing else from your PC. It's built for low delay, not for hiding where you are.

Which games and servers does it work with?

Minecraft Java first, starting with Hypixel and Minemen Club. More servers are planned.

When can I use it?

Rago Route is in testing on Hypixel and will come to Rago Optimizer for Windows. Join the Rago Discord to hear the moment it opens.
