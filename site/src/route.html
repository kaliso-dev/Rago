Why you lag

Your ping looks fine. Your game still freezes.

Lag spikes, rubberbanding, timed out: they all hurt in a fight, but they don't all come from your connection. Pick what you feel: here's what's likely behind it, and whether Rago Route can do something about it.

What's likely happening

A packet got lost on the way.

  1. Your PC
  2. Home network
  3. On the way
  4. Server

Minecraft reads its connection in order. When one packet goes missing, everything after it waits until the lost one is sent again. Then it all lands at once: the freeze, then the jump. Your ping can look fine the whole time.

Built for this

When the packet is lost between your PC and Rago's server in Paris, Rago Route gives it a copy on a second path, a backup packet to rebuild it from, and a quick resend. The gap fills sooner, so your game waits less. If the whole lobby freezes at the same moment, it's the server.

What's likely happening

Your moves reached the server late.

  1. Your PC
  2. Home network
  3. On the way
  4. Server

When the packets carrying your moves get lost or held up, the server sees you late, or all at once, and puts you back where it last saw you. Hypixel's anticheat and a lagging server pull players back too.

Sometimes

Rago Route protects what you send as well as what you receive: your moves get the same copies, backup packets and resends. It can't do anything when the anticheat or the server pulls you back.

How it works

Two paths to Paris. Then the usual road to Hypixel.

01 · On your PC

Your game connects to Rago Route.

Turn Rago Route on in Rago Optimizer, then join localhost in Minecraft instead of the usual address. Nothing is installed in the game.

02 · Two paths

Every packet leaves twice.

One copy goes straight to Rago's server in Paris, the other through Rago's server in Frankfurt. Both start on your Wi-Fi and router: that part is shared, which is why Rago Route also sends backup packets.

03 · In Paris

The first copy to arrive wins.

Rago's server in Paris keeps the first copy of each packet, rebuilds what's missing or gets it sent again, and passes everything on in order. It does the same, the other way, for everything Hypixel sends you.

04 · To Hypixel

Then the usual road to Hypixel.

From Paris, your game crosses to Hypixel's servers in North America like any connection from Europe. Rago Route can't shorten that, and in our test from France it didn't add to it.

05 · Optional

Your phone, as a third path.

Plug in your phone and share its connection before you turn Rago Route on. Its mobile data becomes a path that doesn't go through your Wi-Fi at all.

When a packet gets lost

Three ways back for a lost packet.

They all run at once. Which one brings a packet back depends on where it was lost.

Backup packets cover packets sent close together, one loss per group. A packet sent on its own has no backup packet: its copy and the resend cover it.

When your connection cuts

A short cut doesn't have to end your match.

If your connection drops for a few seconds, Rago's server in Paris keeps your connection to Hypixel open, for up to a minute, while Rago Route waits for your network. In our lab test, after an 8-second cut, traffic was flowing again about 20 ms after the network came back. We haven't tested it in a real match yet.

Minecraft has its own time limits: a long cut can still end your match, with or without Rago Route.

With your phone plugged in, Rago Route keeps a path that doesn't use your Wi-Fi: when the Wi-Fi drops, your game keeps going over mobile data, with a higher ping. USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi hotspot; if the phone is plugged in but not sharing, the Rago Route tab says which step is missing.

In the app

You see what it does. Even when it does nothing.

The Rago Route tab shows each path with its round trip to Paris, how often it arrived first and what it lost, then counts the packets saved by the other path, the packets repaired from backup packets and the resends.

On a clean line, there's nothing to save. In our first test of the app on Windows, nothing was lost, and the tab counted 0 packets saved. Resends can still tick up when a packet is only late.

Is it for you?

Made for lag on the way to Hypixel, not on your PC.

Rago Route is for you if

  • You play Hypixel from Europe, on Windows 10 or 11.
  • Your ping is usually fine, but your game freezes, jumps or pulls you back.
  • Your Wi-Fi or your provider loses packets now and then.
  • Your connection blinks for a second or two, and you'd rather not get kicked.
  • You'd like a backup when your Wi-Fi drops, and your phone plan allows sharing.

It's not the right fit if

  • Your ping is high but steady: that's distance. Hypixel's servers are in North America, about 94 ms from Paris, and nothing shortens that.
  • Your connection is already clean: with nothing lost, there's nothing to save.
  • Your home connection is full, from a download or a stream: both paths start in your router.
  • Your lag comes from low FPS or a lagging server: start with the tweaks.
  • You play from outside Europe, on a Mac, on other servers, or on a network that blocks this traffic (some schools and workplaces).

Questions

Before you try it.

Can Hypixel block my account if I use it?

Hypixel blocks an account when its activity looks unusual, in case it was stolen. With Rago Route, Hypixel sees the address of Rago's server in Paris, which other Rago Route players share. In our test, — accounts played through Rago Route for — hours over — days, and — were blocked. If your account gets blocked, turn Rago Route off and follow Hypixel's steps to get it back.

Will my ping go down?

Usually not, and we don't promise it. Most of your ping is the distance to Hypixel's servers in North America. Rago Route is about the moments when packets get lost. In our first test from France, the median ping didn't go up either.

Will I notice it?

Only when your connection loses packets or cuts out. On a clean line, there's nothing to save, and the Rago Route tab shows 0 packets saved. With light loss, the difference can be hard to feel. We haven't measured yet what it changes in a real match.

Does it help on Wi-Fi?

It can. Rago Route rebuilds and resends the packets your Wi-Fi loses, and keeps your connection open through short cuts. But both of its paths start on your Wi-Fi, so a weak signal slows them both. Plug in a cable if you can. A phone sharing its connection gives your game a path that skips the Wi-Fi.

Is Rago Route a VPN?

Not a full VPN. Only your Minecraft connection goes through it, and only when you join localhost: the rest of your PC's traffic doesn't change. But as with a VPN, Hypixel sees the address of Rago's server in Paris instead of yours.

Why do you need my card for a free trial?

Because the trial becomes the monthly plan unless you cancel. You pay nothing today. The checkout page and the email we send when your trial starts both give the date of the first payment.

How do I cancel the trial?

In two clicks from the app's Settings: Cancel trial, then confirm. You can also cancel from your account page on the site. Do it before the date of the first payment, given in the email we send when your trial starts: you won't be charged, and Premium ends with the trial. After that, Premium renews every month until you cancel. No refunds, except for a double charge or a billing error: see the terms.

What if I forget to cancel?

Your card is charged $5.99 for the first month, 3 days after you start, then every month until you cancel. Payments aren't refunded, so note the date when you start: it's in the email we send and on your account page.

Does it change Minecraft or my client?

No mod, nothing installed in the game. You join localhost instead of the usual address. Rago's server in Paris puts the real server address back in the very first packet, and passes everything else on as it is.

Does it work with Lunar, Badlion or Feather?

Yes. Rago Route doesn't touch the game: you join localhost from your usual client, the same way as from the official launcher.

Is it fair play?

Rago Route carries your game's own packets, encrypted, and delivers them in order. It doesn't change what you send, add anything to the game or touch other players, and it doesn't change how the server counts your hits. Hypixel sees Rago's server in Paris instead of your home connection.

Can Rago see my traffic?

Only your Minecraft connection goes through Rago's server, which passes it on to Hypixel. Its logs keep no IP address and no username, and the pass that opens Rago Route names your license, nothing else.

Which servers does it work with?

Hypixel. And Minemen Club (EU): join localhost:25566. Other servers and other games aren't supported.

Who can use it?

Every Premium account, the free trial included, and Lifetime. Not the free version: Rago's servers only take games from Premium accounts. Already Premium? There's nothing to buy: update Rago Optimizer, then open Network, then Rago Route.

What if Rago's server is down?

Then Rago Route can't connect, and a match going through it stops. Keep your usual Hypixel address in your server list: turn Rago Route off and join it as usual.

Does it use more data?

Yes: every packet travels twice, plus the backup packets. Minecraft uses little data, so on a home connection it doesn't matter. On a phone's mobile data, it counts: in our test, the phone received about 70 MB per hour of play (prototype on a Mac, before backup packets).

Does it work outside Europe?

It's made for players in Europe and tested from France so far: your game goes through Paris. From North America, Hypixel is already close, so the detour wouldn't help.

What does “beta” mean here?

Rago Route is new. We're testing it on more connections, and it can change. The results on this page say exactly what was measured, where and when.
