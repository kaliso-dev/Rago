Why your hits don't register

The server decides. Your timing decides what it sees.

Scroll through what happens between your click and the server.

01 · The journey

Every hit takes a trip.

Your click goes through your mouse, your PC, your network, then the server. The server checks hits on a fixed beat, 20 times a second.

02 · Input lag

Your click leaves faster.

Mouse acceleration makes your aim inconsistent. USB power saving and input queues add delay before your PC even sees the click. Rago turns them off.

03 · Frame time

Your frames stop stuttering.

CPU power states and core parking cause uneven frames. Rago keeps the CPU awake so each frame takes the same time.

04 · Ping jitter

Your timing gets consistent.

The distance to the server doesn't change. What changes is how much your timing jumps around, so more hits land in the beat they were meant for.
