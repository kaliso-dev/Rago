what rago does // in plain words

Every hit takes a trip. Rago makes it shorter.

Your click goes through your mouse, your PC and your network before the server checks it, 20 times a second. Rago tunes the part of that trip that's on your side: Windows, your network settings and Minecraft. It can't move you closer to the server, and it never promises a ping.

All 218 tweaks explained

  • Your click leaves faster.

    Mouse acceleration, USB power saving and input queues add delay before your PC even sees the click. Rago turns them off.

  • Your frames stop stuttering.

    CPU power states and core parking cause uneven frames. Rago keeps the CPU awake so each frame takes the same time.

  • Your timing gets steadier.

    The distance to the server doesn't change. What changes is how much your timing jumps around, so more hits land in the beat they were meant for.

  • Turn it off, it's undone.

    Turn a tweak off and your Windows settings go back the way they were. A restore point is offered before advanced tweaks.

  • Your game stays yours.

    Rago changes Windows and network settings. It doesn't modify Minecraft or any other game and injects nothing into them.
