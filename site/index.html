Why your hits don't register

Ping is only part of the delay.

Between your click and the server, Windows, your network card and your game each add their own wait. Out of the box they're set to save power, not to win fights.

Your input

Mouse acceleration, USB power saving and keyboard delay slow your click before it leaves the desk.

Windows

Background services, telemetry and CPU power states make your game wait its turn.

Network card

Nagle's algorithm, offloads and throttling batch your packets to save bandwidth.

Minecraft

Particles, animated textures and clouds cost frames right when you need them steady.

Distance to server

The physical trip to the server. If your ping comes from distance, no software can shorten it.

Questions

Before you buy.

Can I get banned for using Rago?
Rago doesn't modify Minecraft and injects nothing into the game. It changes Windows settings, your network adapter and your OptiFine options file, the same things you could change by hand. These are system settings, not a cheat.
Will it lower my ping?
It depends where your ping comes from. If you're far from the server, distance can't be fixed. Rago cuts the delay your PC and network add, and makes your ping steadier, which is what matters most for hit registration.
What's the difference between Rago Free and Rago Hub Premium?
Rago Free is the original .bat script with console menus, free and with no account. Rago Hub Premium is the Windows app: all 12 sections in a clean interface, an explanation and risk label on every tweak, before and after measurements, weekly updates and the Premium channel.
Can I use my license on more than one PC?
No. Rago Hub works on one device per license. Once you activate it, your account can't be signed in on another PC. If you change PCs, contact support on Discord.
What if I don't like a change?
A restore point is created before any change, and the Restore section undoes your tweaks. You can go back to how your PC was whenever you want.
Will it work on my PC?
Rago runs on Windows 10 and 11. It has dedicated tweaks for Intel and AMD CPUs, NVIDIA, AMD and Intel GPUs, and a laptop mode.
My antivirus flagged Rago. Why?
Rago changes system and network settings, which is exactly what antivirus tools watch for, so some flag it. Only download Rago from this site. Don't disable your antivirus: allow the file if you trust it, or ask us on Discord.
Why does it ask for administrator rights?
Windows doesn't let any app change network and system settings without them. Without admin rights, part of the tweaks wouldn't apply.
