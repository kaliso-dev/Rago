# Rago voice: English (v1.1)

This replaces the French "Voix" section of the charter for every English surface: the website, the account and licence pages, Rago Optimizer (the app), emails, and Discord announcements. The rest of the charter (logo, colors, type, motion) is unchanged.

**Tagline:** Every millisecond counts.
**Spelling:** US English (optimize, license, color). Sentence case for every heading, button and menu item ("Apply 6 tweaks", not "Apply 6 Tweaks").

## Who we sound like
An experienced competitive player who also knows Windows inside out. We talk to one player at a time.

| Principle | What it means in English |
|---|---|
| Direct | Speak to "you", use contractions (you're, it's, don't). This replaces the French *tutoiement*: friendly and peer-to-peer, never corporate ("Users may…") and never slangy ("bro", "fr fr"). |
| Precise | Say what a tweak does and what it changed, in real units: ms, FPS, %. |
| Calm | No ALL CAPS, no exclamation stacks, no emoji in the product or on the site. One exclamation mark max per screen, for real wins only. |
| Honest | Never promise a number we haven't measured on the player's PC. Use "up to" only with a source. Always say how to undo. |

## Numbers and units
- A space before units (`18 ms`, `243 FPS`), no space before percent (`0%`).
- Deltas use a true minus and plus: `−23 ms`, `+38 FPS`. Green when it's an improvement.
- Prices: `$X.XX` or `€X.XX`, matching the Stripe currency. Always show what's included next to the price.

## Do / don't

## Key copy by surface (starting points, adapt freely)

**Website**
- Hero: "Every millisecond counts." / sub: "Rago tunes Windows, your network and Minecraft for lower ping, steadier FPS and cleaner hitreg. Measured on your PC, undoable in one click."
- Free CTA: "Download for free" + caption "Free plan included. Upgrade anytime."
- Premium CTA: "Get Rago Optimizer Pro" + caption "Secure checkout with Stripe."

**After payment / account**
- Success: "Payment confirmed. Create your account to get your license."
- Sign-in error: "That email and password don't match. Try again or reset your password."
- Download: "Download Rago Optimizer for Windows" + "Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit."

**Licence (one device per account)**
- Activation: "Activate Rago Optimizer on this PC? Your license will be locked to this device."
- Success: "License active. Rago Optimizer is ready."
- Blocked on a second device: "This license is already active on another PC. Rago Optimizer works on one device per license. Contact support on Discord if you changed PCs."

**App**
- Apply button: "Apply 6 tweaks". While running: "Optimizing…" with the Pulse animation. Done: "Applied. Ping −23 ms."
- Before risky changes: "Advanced tweak. It changes a system setting. A restore point is created first."
- Empty dashboard: "Run a measurement to see your baseline ping, jitter and FPS."

## Errors
State what happened, then what to do. No apologies, no blame, no codes without words.
"Couldn't reach the license server. Check your connection and try again." (not "Error 503")
